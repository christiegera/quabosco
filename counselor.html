Counselor's Guide · Overall plan

Run it so they
actually learn it.

Six weekend sessions at home and two camp days, spaced two to three weeks apart. Each boy makes his own film, printed piece, web page, and interview, because every Scout must "actually and personally" complete each requirement.

The one rule to remember: if a requirement says discuss, show, or demonstrate, each boy does it himself. Watching, or staying quiet in a discussion, doesn't count. Guide to Advancement 7.0.3.2 ↗

Before Weekend 1 Set up

  • Second registered adult lined up. With two Scouts present, Scouting America requires two registered leaders. Ask Peter's parent to register and take Youth Protection training, or bring in a troop adult.
  • Michael has talked to the Scoutmaster and has blue cards or Scoutbook entries for all five badges.
  • Peter has talked to the Scoutmaster and has blue cards or Scoutbook entries for his four badges (he already has Moviemaking).
  • Planned Peter's role on the film: he already has Moviemaking, so he crews for Michael (sound, reflector, second camera). His own voiceover clip covers DT 6f.
  • Legion's written OK to film on private property, plus agreement on who owns the photos and video.
  • A Legion contact who will initial volunteer-hour logs.
  • Photo-release plan for anyone identifiable on camera (a parent signs for minors).
  • Rule set with both families: a parent is copied on every message to a Scout.
  • Merit Badge Counselor training is current, and you're re-registered for the year.
  • Both boys have their app link (Michael's and Peter's) saved to their phone's home screen.

    How to counsel well

    1

    Ask the quieter boy first

    Then the other. Don't accept "what he said." A good check: "Explain it like I've never heard of it."

    2

    Explain, Demonstrate, Guide, Enable

    Scouting's EDGE method. By the Enable step, your hands are off the mouse. A rough film they made beats a polished one you fixed.

    3

    Requirements exactly as written

    You can't add, skip, or swap a requirement. If something doesn't fit as written, it doesn't count. Say why kindly and show how to fix it.

    4

    Build planning into the project for Michael

    End every meeting with: what's next, what do I need, by when. He keeps the paper calendar. Give him one clear job per session, then bigger ones as he handles them.

    5

    Same standard for both

    Hold Michael to the same bar as Peter. Date every sign-off and record partials so either boy could finish with another counselor.

    6

    Keep the paper trail

    Keep the Counselor's Record portion of each blue card for at least a year. The Progress page shows sign-off dates to copy onto the cards.
